Happy Birthday, Munazza

Meri sabse khaas insaan, pata hai aaj ka din mere liye kitna khaas hai? Kyunki aaj us insaan ka janamdin hai jiske baare mein sochte hi mere chehre par muskurahat aa jaati hai. ❤️

Tum meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jise main lafzon mein poori tarah bayan nahi kar sakta. Tumhari ek chhoti si baat mera din bana deti hai, aur tumhari ek muskurahat mere dil ko sukoon de deti hai.

Aaj tumhare birthday par main bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki tum meri har dua mein shaamil ho. Main dil se chahta hoon ki tumhari zindagi mein kabhi kisi cheez ki kami na ho. Tumhare har sapne poore hon, har manzil tumhe mile aur tum hamesha khush raho. 🎯

Tumhare saath baat karna, tumhari fikr karna aur tumhe khush dekhna mujhe sach mein bahut pasand hai. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki meri aadat ban gayi ho tum. Din kitna bhi busy ho, ek baar tumhara khayal zaroor aa hi jaata hai. ❤️

Aaj ke din main Allah se bas yahi dua karta hoon ki tumhari zindagi hamesha khushiyon se bhari rahe. Tumhare chehre ki muskurahat kabhi kam na ho, aur tum jis cheez ko dil se chaho, woh tumhe zaroor mile.

Thank you… meri zindagi mein aane ke liye, meri baaton ko sunne ke liye, aur bina kuch kahe bhi mujhe samajh lene ke liye. Tum sach mein mere liye bahut special ho.

Janamdin mubarak ho meri jaan. ❤️ Aaj ka din tumhare naam, aur meri har dua bhi tumhare naam.

I love you more than words can ever explain. ❤️
